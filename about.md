# About

This isn't some highly funded lobbying operation or some startup trying to do
an end run around copyright law - this is a grassroots effort by real consumers
who have been prevented from doing otherwise reasonable things with discs they
own by overbearing DRM. 

And by "grassroots effort" I mean one onery and motivated individual who woke up with an axe to grind one day.

# Will this actually work?

To be honest - all of these claims are long shots, some more so than others. 

But: there are some very encouraging facts that are new this time around though:

1. **4K Discs on PC**: There was a licensed way to play 4K UHD discs on PC that
   completely unravelled since the last rulemaking. This is exactly the scenario
   that motivated Congress to put this process in the DMCA - consumers did everything
   right and followed the licensed pathway, which for reasons completely 
2. **Public Domain**: The public domain clock is ticking again after the Micky Mouse
   Protection Act froze it for years. This means every year there is more public
   domain content on discs protected by DRM that owners can't lawfully access.
3. **4K Drives Aren't Made Anymore**: PC drives that can read 4K discs are out
   of production - LG and Pioneer have called it quits. The number of drives 
   capable of backing up these discs is going to go down as parts wear out
4. **Discs are failing**: There are known defective runs of Blu-ray disks out 
   there - the need for backups of failing discs isn't hypothetical.


# Are you using AI?

All of the copy on this website was written by a real life human. I do not use 
LLMs for communication with other human beings.

However, LLMs are used for:
1. **Legal research**

   I don't have time to read 26 years of meeting transcripts and filings.
   
   Frankly, I consider the ability to force feed all of those documents to an LLM to find citations a key advantage us regular people have this cycle. I can point 
   at damning things a copyright industry lawyer said on the record in 2000.
2. **Drafting filings**

   Most of the arguments and outlines are mine, but LLMs are used to fill it out.
   I just don't have the time to crank out this much writing, and I decided to
   give everything a fair shot instead of concentrating my efforts.

3. **Formatting this website**

   I'm a developer but it is pretty convenient to make a robot do this boilerplate.
