# Summary

Congress considered personal computers an important platform when crafting the
DMCA, but it isn't 1999 anymore. Personal computers aren't just beige boxes
that sit on desks, they're phones, tablets, Chromebooks, consoles and more. 

The computers that most Americans use every day have never had and likely never 
will have a licensed way to play DVDs, Blu-ray, and 4K Blu-ray due to a general
commercial apathy. This is despite those computers being perfectly able to
play those discs back from a technical perspective.

This claim covers both devices with a disc drive directly attached (like via USB)
or a disc drive accessible over the local area network (eg LASERWEB).
