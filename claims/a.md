# Summary

Playing 4K UHD Blu-ray discs on PC required specific Intel processors, and was
entirely broken in 2022 when Intel deprecated SGX. Consumers who bought into the
4K ecosystem and had legal discs, players, and software were left out in the cold.

Congress allows the Library of Congress to allow circumventing DRM for exactly
this situation: through a tangled web of commercial disinterest, suddenly 
consumers are unable to play the media that they paid for. 

This claim seeks to fix that.
