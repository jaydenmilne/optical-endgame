# Summary

Congress passed a whole law in 2005 making it clear that filtering media according to
the owner's values is not copyright infringement. Unfortunately in 2026 there
isn't actually a way for owners to filter discs, there is no licensed pathway
with this functionality. 

Owners should be allowed to bypass DRM if it is the only thing preventing them
from filtering content - an explicitly legal thing to do.
