# Summary

Often discs are sold with a bundled code to allow streaming playback on devices
without a licensed way to play the disc. Unfortunantly, studios have revoked
those codes and prevented people from accessing their digital copy. They've even
done this after redeeming codes.

This claim aims to allow owners to make a digital copy if they are unable to 
redeem a code or if a studio later revokes their code. A deal is a deal.
