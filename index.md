<div class="site-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">The Clock Is Ticking</p>
  <h1>It's time to allow owners to play their disks</h1>
  <p class="hero-deck">The Library of Congress is considering exemptions to 
  Document Rights Management (DRM). DRM is what prevents you from playing your 
  movies on your computer, mobile phone, and making backup copies. </p>
  <p class="hero-deck">The Library of Congress only considers new exceptions (under Section 1201(a)(1) of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act) every three years. It's time someone stood up for consumers. The clock is ticking on PC-based optical media.</p>

  <p> WE NEED YOUR HELP!</p>

  <p>To make a persuasive case we have to give examples of real consumers who have
  been harmed by DRM</p>

  <p>If you have a story about any of the following <b>please email us at opticalendgame@jayd.ml</b></p>

* Did you ever play Ultra High Definition (UHD) 4K Blu-rays on PC via CyberLink
    PowerDVD or  Corel WinDVD UDH BD?
* Do you own disks that you can't play on your computer without VLC - like on
    ChromeOS, SteamOS, Linux, Mac OS, Android, iOS, iPad OS?
* Do you have disks that are actively failing where you need to make a personal backup?
* Do you own a disk drive that is out of production - like a PC 4K UHD Blu-ray drive,
    DVD drive, or HD-DVD drive?
* Do you have movies with explicit content that you'd like to filter - like using Clearplay or Vidangel?
* Do you own a movie on disk that is in the public domain or about to enter the public 
  domain you'd like to copy?
* Do you need a short clip from a 4K UHD Blu-ray to make content (video essay, documentary, YouTube video)?

<b>Please email us at opticalendgame@jayd.ml!</b>

We need your story! This will only work if we can get enough compelling
stories to persuade the Library of Congress.
