# What We Hope To Accomplish

**The goal of this project is not to enable piracy.** It is to allow lawful, 
reasonable use of media that consumers have already purchased. If you just want
free movies, look elsewhere. Without copyright artists don't get paid and everything
from indie movies to $250 million dollar blockbusters don't get made. We are trying to work within and develop copyright 
law here.

That being said, Congress allowed the Library of Congress to authorize circumvention 
of DRM for the exact situation optical media finds itself in. A lack of commercial
interest has broken DRM schemes on previously fully licensed pathways (4K UHD on PC),
and worse has failed to allow the computers most people own in 2026 (tablets, phones, 
Chromebooks) to lawfully play movies.

Worse, these discs are aging and the drives to play them on computers are out
of production. There is no way for owners to ensure they can play back their
media for decades to come.
