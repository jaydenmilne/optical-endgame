# CLAIM A — ULTRA HD BLU-RAY PC PLAYBACK

## Item B. Description of Proposed New Exemption

### 1. Types of copyrighted works

Motion pictures, including television programs, videos, and supplementary motion-picture works, distributed on Ultra HD Blu-ray Disc and protected by AACS2.

### 2. Physical media and devices

The works are stored on lawfully made and lawfully acquired Ultra HD Blu-ray Discs. The requested activity is playback directly from the disc in a compatible optical drive on an owner-controlled general-purpose personal computer. The disc would remain the source throughout playback.

### 3. Purpose of access

The purpose is contemporaneous, private, local, noncommercial playback. Playback would use only transitory technical data and would leave no complete or substantially complete decrypted motion picture reasonably reconstructable after the session. The requested activity does not include making or retaining a movie file, later playback from a copy, distribution, remote access, transmission, public performance, or a commercial service.

### 4. Users seeking access

The users are individual owners in continuous lawful possession of the disc who, after reasonable configuration efforts, cannot complete the claimed playback through a licensed, operational, reasonably available U.S. consumer pathway. A pathway must be currently obtainable and usable by an ordinary U.S. consumer on the computer used for the claimed playback; an obsolete listing, unavailable legacy product, or theoretical license is not sufficient.

### 5. Existing and likely barriers

Ultra HD Blu-ray Discs in the proposed class use AACS2, which AACS LA identifies as the content-protection technology licensed for Ultra HD Blu-ray. Identified vendor-supported PC pathways have contracted through changes that took effect after the August 25, 2023 deadline for petitions in the prior proceeding:

- Corel stated that the Ultra HD Blu-ray playback function in its OEM WinDVD UHD BD product, supplied with certain PCs and Blu-ray Disc drives, was expected to become unavailable beginning October 1, 2023.
- CyberLink states that, as of November 2023, Ultra HD Blu-ray playback is no longer supported in PowerDVD 20, 21, 22, and 365; CyberLink says it ceased both the playback feature and corresponding online authentication after October 2023, making Ultra HD Blu-ray Discs unplayable in those products.
- Intel announced that all active API versions of its SGX Attestation Service using EPID would reach end of life on April 2, 2025. CyberLink states that, as of April 2025, the resulting termination left Ultra HD Blu-ray playback unavailable on Windows PC platforms, including older SGX-capable systems and previously supporting PowerDVD versions.

These vendor statements document announced or completed withdrawals of identified pathways; the current U.S. product census and claimant-specific testing will be supplied in Round 1. On each qualifying configuration, the disc, drive, and every element of the playback path other than AACS2 must otherwise function, leaving AACS2 as the remaining source-disc access barrier. Circumventing AACS2 would supply the decryption needed for direct playback, but owners refrain because § 1201(a)(1)(A) prohibits that act. But for the prohibition, they could make the bounded private performance described above without retaining a copy or adding an audience.
